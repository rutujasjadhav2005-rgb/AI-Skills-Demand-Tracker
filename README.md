# 📊 AI Skills Demand Tracker – Tableau Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **AI Skills Demand Tracker** is an interactive data visualization project developed using Tableau. The project analyzes AI job market trends, identifies the most in-demand skills, and explores salary insights across different job categories and experience levels.

The main objective is to understand the growing demand for AI-related skills and provide meaningful insights into the evolving AI job market.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze demand for AI-related job roles.
- Identify the most in-demand AI skills.
- Understand salary trends across different skills.
- Compare AI job demand by experience level.
- Analyze AI job postings across different countries.
- Explore salary premiums and demand growth.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Tableau:** Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV:** Dataset handling.
- **Data Analytics:** Data exploration and analysis.

## 📂 Project Dashboards

This project contains two interactive dashboards:

### 1. AI Skills Demand Tracker
- Total AI Job Postings
- Total AI Skills
- Top In-Demand Skills
- AI Job Demand by Category
- AI Job Demand by Experience Level
- Top Countries by AI Job Postings

### 2. AI Skills & Salary Insights
- Average AI Salary
- Average Demand Growth
- AI Skills vs Salary
- AI Skills Demand Growth
- Average AI Salary by Experience Level
- Top 10 Skills Required for Generative AI & LLM Roles
